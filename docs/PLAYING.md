# Playing the frontier — 0.4.0

## Start and customize

Choose one of fourteen classes. Each has two active abilities; the class hall lets you try other classes while keeping gear, levels, gold and quest progress. Switching starts a new run.

Open **Hero** to choose race, faction, skin/hair, accent and face. All races can play every class and follow either faction. Race changes the model; faction changes narrative allegiance and camp banners, without combat restrictions in this alpha. Equipped gear appears on your hero.

## Adventure and exploration

Adventure uses the main screen as the 3D viewport. **ROAM** enters exploration, pausing combat. Use the north/east/south/west movement pad, or **MAP** to select a local destination and walk there automatically. Movement directions follow world directions in both camera views.

**3RD** switches to the following third-person camera. **SKY** returns to an aerial view, lifting the camera during the transition. You can continue walking in either mode. **FIGHT** resumes the idle expedition; it does not require clearing the local map.

The map shows your hero in blue, ordinary sites in pale colors, elites in gold, camps in green and the boss in red. Every site shows its level requirement. A new seeded arrangement appears each run; the same saved run keeps the same arrangement.

Approach a site and use its interaction button:

- **Rest at camp:** fully restore health, without XP or gold.
- **Open cache:** collect a small one-time XP/gold reward for this run.
- **Fight nearby enemy:** start that site's encounter. Elites have 35% more health, 20% more incoming damage and 1.5× XP/gold.
- **Boss site:** opens at encounter 10, after nine victories in the run. Leaving for exploration does not remove the gate requirement.

Clearing a chosen site marks it and returns you to exploration. Run choices at encounters 3, 6 and 9 pause combat for a blessing. **LOG** opens the expedition journal, explanations, abilities and victory rewards. Fresh runs preserve permanent progress, clear temporary blessings and regenerate sites.

## Tactics and damage

Choose **LEFT**, **CENTRE** or **RIGHT** to move your hero into that combat lane. Later bosses announce a dangerous lane and turns until impact. Move away before impact: dodging the special halves its ordinary incoming damage; standing in the marked lane applies its damage multiplier.

Tap the stance button to cycle:

- **Balanced:** normal outgoing/incoming damage.
- **Assault:** +20% damage dealt and received.
- **Guard:** −15% damage dealt, −30% damage received.

Use class spells manually or enable **AUTO**. Cooldowns advance only during active combat; they freeze while paused, exploring or choosing a blessing. Floating numbers distinguish outgoing damage, red incoming hits, critical hits, green healing, blue blocks and dodges.

## Gear, company and progression

Open **Shop → Companion lodge** to hire allies with earned gold. One follows you at a time. Damage allies contribute to your attack, defensive allies add armor and Lumen Wisp restores health every fourth combat turn. Hired companions remain yours across runs and class changes; resting or switching them costs nothing.

The shop also contains introductory gear and the **Regional armoury**, filterable by region and slot. Regional items require that region's level. New loot shows its origin, material, lore, item level and required level. Rarity frames are white, green, blue, purple and orange. Item cards compare current-slot attack/health. Some advanced jewelry adds critical chance (combined cap 20%) or regeneration (combined cap 5 HP per combat turn).

Eight regions unlock at levels 1, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 and 28. Story rewards are claimed in order from **Quests**. Daily quests reset at 00:00 UTC. Offline XP/gold accrues at one ordinary regional reward per 30 seconds, up to eight hours per absence; it never awards loot, quest kills or boss wins.

## Settings and saves

Enable original music in **Settings**. Sound effects have their own switch. Low graphics reduces scenery/particle counts and disables antialiasing. Scenes and audio suspend in the background.

Saves are local. Install updates over the existing app; removing the app deletes progress. Saves from 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 migrate automatically in 0.4.0. Camera, heritage, faction, companions and current world position are saved too.
