# Class guide — 0.3.0

Fourteen original class kits. Each has two active abilities, a class weapon, separate cooldowns and optional auto-casting. Cooldowns advance during combat only.

## Bastion (Warrior)

A steadfast guardian who turns steel into shelter.

- **Shield Bash** — Strike for 160% attack and interrupt one enemy turn. Cooldown: 9 combat turns.
- **Iron Wall** — Absorb damage equal to 24% max HP for four turns. Cooldown: 14 combat turns.

## Starweaver (Mage)

A stargazer who shapes embers and falling frost.

- **Ember Lance** — Deal 220% attack as fire damage. Cooldown: 8 combat turns.
- **Frost Nova** — Deal 120% attack and freeze one enemy turn. Cooldown: 12 combat turns.

## Duskblade (Rogue)

An agile treasure hunter with a venomous edge.

- **Venom Edge** — Deal 100% attack, then 30% each turn for four turns. Cooldown: 10 combat turns.
- **Smoke Step** — Strike for 120% attack and evade one enemy turn. Cooldown: 11 combat turns.

## Clockbinder (Bard)

A wandering musician whose songs mend the broken dawn.

- **Resonant Chord** — Deal 170% attack as a resonating wave. Cooldown: 8 combat turns.
- **Mending Ballad** — Restore 20% max HP. Cooldown: 14 combat turns.

## Dawnwarden (Paladin)

An oathbound knight carrying the last light.

- **Dawn Smite** — Deal 180% attack in radiant damage. Cooldown: 9 combat turns.
- **Radiant Aegis** — Heal 10% max HP and gain a 14% HP barrier for four turns. Cooldown: 15 combat turns.

## Thornstrider (Ranger)

A patient archer who binds prey with living thorns.

- **Piercing Arrow** — Fire a shot dealing 210% attack. Cooldown: 9 combat turns.
- **Binding Vines** — Deal 80% attack and root one enemy turn. Cooldown: 12 combat turns.

## Lightkeeper (Cleric)

A gentle healer whose prayers can also burn.

- **Sacred Bolt** — Deal 170% attack in holy damage. Cooldown: 8 combat turns.
- **Renewal** — Restore 26% max HP. Cooldown: 16 combat turns.

## Riftcaller (Warlock)

A pact mage trading certainty for forbidden power.

- **Rift Hex** — Deal 100% attack, then 35% each turn for four turns. Cooldown: 11 combat turns.
- **Drain Essence** — Deal 120% attack and restore 12% max HP. Cooldown: 13 combat turns.

## Cloudfist (Monk)

A calm martial artist moving with the mountain wind.

- **Twin Strike** — Release a focused flurry for 200% attack. Cooldown: 9 combat turns.
- **Still Water** — Heal 15% max HP and gain a 10% HP barrier for three turns. Cooldown: 14 combat turns.

## Stormspeaker (Shaman)

A keeper of storms and ancient earth spirits.

- **Storm Bolt** — Deal 190% attack as lightning damage. Cooldown: 8 combat turns.
- **Earth Ward** — Absorb damage equal to 22% max HP for four turns. Cooldown: 13 combat turns.

## Ashreaver (Berserker)

A fearless axe fighter tempered by the ashlands.

- **Crushing Blow** — Deal 230% attack with a heavy axe swing. Cooldown: 10 combat turns.
- **Blood Fury** — Deal 130% attack and restore 10% max HP. Cooldown: 13 combat turns.

## Gearwright (Artificer)

An inventor armed with unstable crystal technology.

- **Arc Charge** — Discharge a crystal blast for 200% attack. Cooldown: 9 combat turns.
- **Field Repair** — Heal 15% max HP and gain a 12% HP barrier for four turns. Cooldown: 15 combat turns.

## Wildheart (Shapeshifter)

A wild guardian who becomes fang, fur and forest.

- **Feral Pounce** — Become a wolf for five turns and strike for 200% attack. Cooldown: 10 combat turns.
- **Bear Form** — Become a bear for six turns and gain a 25% HP barrier. Cooldown: 15 combat turns.

## Gravewhisper (Necromancer)

A soulbinder who bends spectral echoes to protect the living.

- **Soul Scythe** — Deal 180% attack as spectral damage. Cooldown: 8 combat turns.
- **Bone Ward** — Gain a 15% HP barrier and curse for 25% attack over four turns. Cooldown: 14 combat turns.
